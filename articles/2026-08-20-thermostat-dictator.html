Local Man Discovers Thermostat, Immediately Becomes Dictator of Household Climate

Kevin Marsh, 34, has reportedly seized unilateral control over his apartment's HVAC system after discovering the thermostat is not, in fact, controlled by a wizard or the government but is just a small box on the wall that anyone can touch.

"I always thought it was decorative," Kevin told reporters while standing guard in front of the small beige rectangle like a dragon guarding its hoard. "Like a little temperature painting. Turns out you can just push the buttons and the air changes. I felt like Zeus. I felt like I had finally unlocked the third chakra of my apartment."

Witnesses say Kevin spent approximately nine minutes pressing the up arrow, then the down arrow, then the up arrow again, before settling on 71 degrees Fahrenheit, a number he described as "the perfect temperature, the one God would choose if God paid rent in Southern California."

His girlfriend, Amanda Reyes, confirmed the regime change. "He came into the bedroom and said, 'I control the climate now.' I said, 'What?' He said, 'The climate. It answers to me.' Then he adjusted it two degrees and spent the next hour explaining the difference between 70 and 72 like he was defending a dissertation."

Amanda noted that the thermostat had been set to 72 for the past three years without incident. Kevin insists the previous setting was "basically an ice age" and that his reforms have "saved the household ecosystem from collapse."

"He keeps walking past it and just looking at it," she added. "Like it's a Rubik's Cube he already solved but keeps checking. Last night he touched it six times in one hour. Three of those were just to confirm it was still set to what he set it to."

Kevin has since established several policies, including a "No Cold Hands on the Dial" rule, a mandatory household temperature briefing each morning, and a strategic reserve of 69 degrees that he claims is "for emergencies only, not for guests, and definitely not for you, Derek," referring to his roommate who has reportedly been wearing a hoodie indoors since Tuesday.
