Local Man Discovers 'Outside,' Files Emergency Patent

By Staff Reporter | SpoofDaily News Desk

Kevin Marsh, 34, of Dayton, Ohio, has done what no human in recorded history has ever accomplished: he walked out his front door on a Tuesday morning and discovered something he is now calling "the outside." His lawyer confirmed the emergency patent filing was submitted at 9:47 AM, roughly eleven minutes after the initial discovery.

"I always thought the world ended at the mailbox," Marsh told reporters while standing in his driveway, squinting violently. "Turns out there is a whole street back here. And behind that, apparently, more street. It just keeps going. I got scared and came back inside for a snack, but then I went out again. It is addictive."

The patent, which has been reviewed by absolutely nobody qualified to review it, claims ownership of "the general concept of being outdoors, including but not limited to sky, grass, and that weird feeling when wind hits your face." Legal experts say the filing has approximately zero chance of being granted, which is roughly the same number of hours Marsh has spent outdoors in the past calendar year prior to this incident.

His roommate, Derek Voll, described the moment of discovery with the kind of reverence usually reserved for moon landings. "He opened the door to check if his pizza delivery had arrived. It had not. But he kept walking. I thought he was dead. I texted his mom. She said he used to do this as a kid and she thought he grew out of it."

Within hours of the patent filing, Big Tech had already responded. A spokesperson for Meta said the company was "monitoring the situation" and "exploring whether Outside could be integrated into existing metaverse experiences as a premium add-on." Google declined to comment but was seen purchasing the entire state of Ohio. Apple announced a $3,499 pair of sunglasses called Outside Pro, featuring a proprietary anti-sunlight coating and a detachable chin strap that nobody asked for or wants.

Meanwhile, Twitter users began sharing their own "outside" stories using the hashtag #OutsideChallenge, which quickly devolved into people posting photos of their backyards while arguing about whose sky was superior. One viral thread featured a woman in Phoenix claiming her sun was "objectively more golden" than the sun in Cleveland. She received fourteen thousand replies calling her a liar and a bot.

Marsh himself remains cautiously optimistic. "I am not saying I invented outside," he clarified, adjusting a pair of safety goggles he found in the garage. "I am saying I was the first person to realize it could be monetized. There is a difference. My lawyer says so."

His lawyer, contacted for comment, said, "Please stop calling this number."

At press time, Marsh was seen back inside, watching a YouTube tutorial titled "How to Walk on Grass: A Beginner's Guide" and taking furious notes. He has also purchased a book about not going outside, which he says he plans to read "ironically, outside."
